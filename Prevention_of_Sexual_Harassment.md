# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## Question 1: What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes unwanted behaviour that makes others feel uncomfortable or unsafe.

- Making unwanted comments or jokes.
- Touching someone without permission.
- Sending inappropriate messages or photos.
- Making repeated requests for dates.
- Passing unwanted comments about someone's appearance.

## Question 2: What would you do if you face or witness any incident of such behaviour?

- I would tell the person to stop if I feel it is safe.
- I would support the affected person and listen to them without judging.
- I would report the incident to HR or the Internal Committee (IC).
- I would keep any proof, such as messages, emails or screenshots.
- I would not ignore such behaviour, even if it happens to someone else.
- I would respect the person's privacy and help maintain a safe workplace.
