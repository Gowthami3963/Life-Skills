# Grit and Growth Mindset

#### Question 1
**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in 1 or 2 lines using your own words.**

Grit means having passion for our goals and working continuously to achieve them. Success depends not only on talent but also on dedication and consistent effort.

#### Question 2
**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in 1 or 2 lines using your own words.**

A growth mindset means believing that we can improve our skills through learning and practice. We should accept challenges, learn from mistakes, and never give up easily.

#### Question 3
**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions and decisions influence our results. We should take responsibility for our actions instead of blaming others or situations.

#### Question 4
**What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a Growth Mindset?**

1. Believe in your ability to figure things out.
2. Question your assumptions.
3. Create your own curriculum for growth.
4. Honor the struggle.

#### Question 5
**What are your ideas to take action and build a Growth Mindset?**

- I will try to learn new concepts on my own instead of depending on others.
