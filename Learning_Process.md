# Learning Process

## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where we explain complex concepts in simple words to check whether we truly understand them.

## Question 2: In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting idea for me was that if we cannot understand a subject even after studying hard, we need to change our learning methods and approaches. We can improve our understanding through continuous effort and practice.

## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

**Focused Mode:** We concentrate on a particular task without distractions. For example, solving a coding problem.

**Diffuse Mode:** It is a relaxed state of thinking where our mind is free from pressure and can explore different ideas. For example, taking a break after studying continuously for one hour.

## Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.

1. Break the skill or topic into smaller parts.
2. Learn enough to start practicing and correct your own mistakes.
3. Remove distractions that stop you from learning.
4. Practice for at least 20 hours and don't give up when it feels difficult.
